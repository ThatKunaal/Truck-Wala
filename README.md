# 🚛 Truck Wala — Songs from the Highway

### 🎵 A little collection of songs for long Indian highway journeys.

🌐 **Live Website:**  
https://thatkunaal.github.io/Truck-Wala/

---

## 🛣️ About The Project

**Truck Wala** is a creative music-player website inspired by the atmosphere of Indian highways, trucks, roadside dhabas, and long journeys.

The idea is simple:

> Get on the highway, turn up the volume, and let the journey play.

The website focuses on creating a nostalgic and immersive visual experience while keeping the music controls minimal and simple.

---

## 🎵 Features

- 🎧 Local music playback
- 🔀 Random song selection
- 🚫 No immediate song repetition
- 🔄 New shuffle cycle after all songs are played
- ⏮️ Previous song
- ▶️ Play / Pause
- ⏭️ Next song
- 📊 Interactive progress bar
- 🖼️ Song-specific thumbnails
- 🎼 Song title and artist information
- 🌄 Fixed highway-inspired background
- 📱 Responsive design
- 🎨 Minimal and aesthetic interface

---

## 🎮 Controls

| Control | Function |
|--------|----------|
| ⏮️ Previous | Go back to the previously played song |
| ▶️ / ⏸️ Play | Play or pause the current song |
| ⏭️ Next | Play another random song |

---

## 🔀 How Shuffle Works

The player uses a **no-repeat shuffle system**.

For example:

```text
Song 1
   ↓
Song 5
   ↓
Song 2
   ↓
Song 7
   ↓
Song 3
   ↓
Song 6
   ↓
Song 4
   ↓
New Shuffle Cycle
